How it's calculated
  • Direction: target above the open price = long, below = short.
  • Account balance = deposit + investment (account currency).
  • Position size = investment converted to USD × leverage (in units: ÷ open price).
  • Margin level = equity ÷ used margin. Equity = balance + P/L. Used margin = position value at the current price ÷ leverage (that's how XTB recalculates it).
  • Margin call / stop-out happen when the margin level drops to the % you set. On XTB a margin call is only a warning; at stop-out (usually 50 %) positions get closed. Check the exact levels for your account.
  • Currency: P/L and margin are converted to your account currency at the same rate (like XTB), so the rate changes the money amounts and the position size, but not where the lines are. The rate is the ECB daily reference rate; XTB uses its own live rate, so small differences are normal.

Not included: other open positions on the same account, swap/overnight fees (they slowly eat your deposit), spread, exchange-rate moves after you open (if your currency weakens against USD, the lines move closer), rollover gaps on futures-based CFDs, and price gaps — price can jump straight past the line.
